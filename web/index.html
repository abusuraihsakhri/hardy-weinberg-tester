Interpretation and limitations

The exact test conditions on observed allele counts and is preferable with sparse expected genotype counts. The chi-square approximation uses one degree of freedom.

A p-value greater than 0.05 means failure to reject equilibrium, not proof of equilibrium. Deviations are not evidence of any specific mechanism without additional analysis.

Scope: autosomal diploid biallelic counts, independent observations. This tool does not adjust for population stratification, relatedness, sex-linked loci, or genotyping uncertainty.

Calculations execute using hwe.py through Pyodide in your browser. Runtime assets load from jsDelivr. Nothing is uploaded by this application. Source and usage.
